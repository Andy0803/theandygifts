What the Charred Wood Actually Does

The tumbler is handcrafted from premium acacia confusa (Janka hardness: 1,700 lbf — denser than most hardwoods, built to resist cracking) and individually charred with a 1300°C flame. The charring process mimics barrel ageing: the charred layer releases trace notes of vanilla and caramel into the drink, adding a subtle warmth that a glass or stainless steel tumbler simply cannot replicate. Combined with acacia wood's natural insulating properties — keeping drinks cold 1.5× longer than glass, 2.5× longer than stainless steel — the result is a drink that tastes more considered and stays cold longer.

The flavour contribution is real but subtle. This isn't a glass that overwhelms a good bourbon — it complements it. The man who has opinions about his whiskey will notice it. The man who just wants a cold drink on a Friday will appreciate that it stays cold.

The One Rule He Needs to Know

Hand wash only. Rinse with warm water, dry thoroughly, store upright. No dishwasher — ever. One dishwasher cycle will warp the wood, damage the charred interior, and void the Tomzio lifetime warranty. This is the single piece of information worth passing on when you give it: the care is simple, but it's non-negotiable. If he's the type who looks after things he values, this will last for years. If everything goes in the dishwasher regardless, consider a different gift.
